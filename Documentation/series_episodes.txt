WEBGL FUNDAMENTALS — EPISODE GUIDE
==================================

This guide covers the lessons in this repository, from Chapter 0 through
Chapter 20. The animation, physics, and collision curriculum continues in the
Sckorpio Real-Time WebGL repository:
https://github.com/SckorpioGraphics/sckorpio-real-time-webgl

CHAPTER 0 — SERIES INTRODUCTION
-------------------------------
Introduces the series and its goals.

SUPPLEMENTAL EXPERIMENTS
------------------------
`chapters/chapterExtra/chapterExtra1.js` explores an animated line and render
loop. `chapters/chapterExtra/chapterExtra2.js` renders random pixel-space
rectangles.

PHASE 1 — WEBGL FOUNDATIONS
===========================
Chapter 1 — First WebGL draw: create a WebGL 2 context, compile shaders, and
draw a triangle.
Chapter 2 — The lesson template: introduce the series' object-centered
organization and data-plus-behavior principle.
Chapter 3 — Geometry and indexing: draw triangles, rectangles, and indexed
geometry.
Chapter 4 — Points, lines, and outlines: use points, line segments, strips,
and loops.
Chapter 5 — Triangle topologies: compare triangles, strips, and fans.
Chapter 6 — Dynamic geometry: update and generate vertex data.
Chapter 7 — Vertex data and color: store, interleave, and interpolate colors.
Chapter 8 — Uniforms: control shader values and colors from JavaScript.
Chapter 9 — Multiple objects: organize objects, shaders, buffers, and draw
calls.

PHASE 2 — 2D COORDINATES AND TRANSFORMATIONS
============================================
Chapter 10 — 2D space and camera: map pixel coordinates to clip space and
introduce camera bounds.
Chapter 11 — Projection matrix: build and apply a projection matrix.
Chapter 12 — View and camera control: add camera state, zoom, and keyboard
movement.
Chapter 13 — Translation: move geometry directly and with matrices.
Chapter 14 — Rotation: rotate objects and explore transformation order and
pivots.
Chapter 15 — Scale: apply uniform and non-uniform scaling.
Chapter 16 — Model matrix: compose per-object translation, rotation, and scale.

PHASE 3 — TEXTURES AND ALPHA
============================
Chapter 17 — Textures: create, reuse, and apply textures.
Chapter 18 — Texture coordinates: map UVs and explore wrapping.
Chapter 19 — Texture filtering and mipmaps: compare filtering at different
scales.
Chapter 20 — Alpha and blending: introduce alpha, blending, compositing, and
transparent textures.

RUNNING
-------
Serve the repository root over HTTP and open http://localhost:8000:

    python3 -m http.server 8000

The default lesson is Chapter 20h. Change its module path in index.html to
select another lesson.
